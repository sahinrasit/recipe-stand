Privacy Policy

Recipe Stand · Last updated 12 October 2026

SupportTürkçe

Short version: Recipe Stand has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your recipes stay on your device. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

What stays on your device

  • Recipes, photos, shopping lists and settings are stored locally on your iPhone or iPad. They are included in your own device backups (for example iCloud Backup) under your Apple Account, which we cannot access.
  • Voice commands are recognised with Apple's on-device speech recognition. Audio is not recorded, stored or sent to us or anyone else.
  • Text recognition of screenshots, photos and scanned pages happens on your device with Apple's Vision framework.
  • Importing from a link downloads that web page directly from your device, the same way Safari would. We don't see which links you import.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. To understand sales, the app reports purchase information (an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID, the product purchased, price, currency, country/storefront and transaction dates) to RevenueCat, Inc., our purchase-analytics provider. This information is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking or advertising. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Microphone & Speech Recognition — only while you turn on voice commands in Cook Mode.
  • Camera — only when you scan a page.
  • Photos — only the images you pick to import.
  • Notifications — to alert you when a kitchen timer finishes.

You can change these at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Children

Recipe Stand is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

Contact

Questions about privacy: mrsahinrasit@gmail.com. If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
